## Risk themes for counsel

### 1. `Agentick` without a space is the closest practical concern

`Agent Tick` collapses visually and phonetically toward `Agentick` in URLs, package names, bundle IDs, and informal speech. The observed `agenticklabs/agentick` project is also AI/developer-tool adjacent. Counsel should check whether any owner is using `Agentick` commercially, whether applications exist outside the public mirrors searched here, and whether coexistence risk is acceptable.

Recommended owner action before filing:

- capture current screenshots of the `agenticklabs/agentick` homepage/repo and GitHub organization
- check npm/PyPI/package registries for `agentick` and close variants
- ask counsel whether `Agent Tick` should file as words only, stylized, or both

### 2. `Tick` is not unique in apps, tasks, and agent coordination

`TickTick` is an established task-management/mobile app mark. `TICK.md` and `tick-md` use `Tick` in an agent/workflow context. These do not appear to be exact approval-routing products, but they mean `Tick` alone is likely weak or crowded.

Recommended owner action:

- avoid marketing that suggests ownership of `Tick` by itself
- use the full `Agent Tick` name consistently
- keep attribution to `Self-Deprecated` visible in footer/legal copy

### 3. AI `Agent...` naming is crowded

Search results show multiple AI/software marks beginning with `Agent`, including `AGENTIC`, `AGENTORY`, and `AGENT.SO`. That makes broad exclusivity around the `Agent` prefix unlikely and increases the importance of precise product descriptions.

Recommended owner action:

- avoid claims like “the agent approval app” as a mark
- describe the product functionally as least-permission approvals for coding agents
- keep product claims narrow: bounded approvals, local execution, no remote shell

## Launch-safe interim guidance

Until counsel reviews final clearance:

- Use `Agent Tick` as a product name with `Self-Deprecated` attribution.
- Do not use ® or claim a registered trademark.
- Avoid shortening the product to `Tick` in headings, app-store metadata, package names, or social handles.
- Avoid `Agentick` as a spelling in public copy except where required by technical identifiers already in use, such as `agenttick.sh`.
- Preserve records of first use, launch pages, package releases, app submissions, and source-control history.
- Treat the checkmark logo/trade dress as a separate clearance question from the word mark.

## Counsel follow-up checklist

Ask counsel to review:

1. Whether `Agent Tick` is sufficiently distinctive for the relevant software/SaaS/mobile classes.
2. Whether `agenticklabs/agentick` or other `Agentick` uses create a refusal or confusion risk.
3. Whether `TickTick` creates meaningful app-store or trademark risk given the mobile app and productivity/task-management adjacency.
4. Whether to file a word mark, stylized mark, logo/checkmark mark, or staged filings.
5. Whether use of `agenttick.sh`, `app.agenttick.sh`, package scopes, bundle IDs, and app-store names should be adjusted before launch.
6. Whether international clearance is needed before app-store launch outside the United States.
7. Whether any disclaimers or attribution should appear in website terms, app-store metadata, or docs.
